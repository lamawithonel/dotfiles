## Copy

Every pen is a small courtroom. You are the only witness, and the only verdict that matters is the one you're willing to sign your name to. Most decisions get spoken and forgotten by evening; the ones written down outlive the room they were made in. That is not a feature of ink. That is the whole of it.

If you don't trust your own memory, trust your own hand. If you don't like who you were this morning, write who you'll be by noon -- the page does not argue, it only holds. A pen does not make you certain. It makes your certainty impossible to take back.

Buy the pen. Not because it writes well. Because some things, once written, cannot be unmeant.
