## Copy

A pen is not a tool. A pen is a decision that outlives the hand that held it.

Ink does not fade. Memory fades. Ink is what you reach for when you refuse to let memory win that argument.

You do not write with a pen. You write against forgetting, and forgetting always loses to a pen that is still in the room.

If you don't like how the world remembers you, change what you put in writing. If you don't like what you put in writing, change the instrument that decides how hard the truth presses into the page.

A signature is not your name. A signature is a fact you have made permanent about yourself, on purpose, in front of witnesses who were not even there.

The paper does not care what you meant. The paper only keeps what you committed. Commit.

You will write one true sentence this year that changes everything after it. You do not know which sentence it is yet. The pen does not need to know either -- it only needs to be in your hand when it happens.

Weight is character. A pen too light forgets itself mid-stroke; a pen with weight remembers, every time, that it is making a mark and not leaving a smudge.

Every contract that mattered, every vow, every will, every apology worth keeping, was signed, not spoken. Speech dissolves in the air that carries it. Ink stays exactly where you leave it -- it tastes like consequence, it sounds like a door closing, it smells like the second you stopped being able to take it back.

You are not choosing a writing instrument. You are choosing which version of you gets to be the permanent one.

If you don't like the draft, revise the draft. If you don't like the person the draft describes, pick up the pen and describe someone truer.

The hand shakes before the truth arrives. The pen does not care that the hand shakes. It only cares that the hand, eventually, presses down anyway.

There is a page somewhere, right now, waiting on you specifically -- not on your typing, not on your intentions, on the exact pressure of this pen in this hand tonight.

You will not remember buying this pen. You will remember what you signed with it, what you promised with it, what you finally admitted with it at an hour no one else was awake to witness.

Nothing is settled until it is signed. Nothing is signed until you press.

Press.
