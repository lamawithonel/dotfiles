## Copy

Here is the moment nobody warns you about: the form is signed by everyone
else, the room is waiting, and your hand is empty. A pen is not ink in a
tube -- it is the one tool that turns your name from a thing you say into a
thing that's true.

Every signature you've ever made was a small bet that you would still be
you tomorrow. This is the tool that lets you keep making it. Take it. Sign
what matters, and let it be done.
