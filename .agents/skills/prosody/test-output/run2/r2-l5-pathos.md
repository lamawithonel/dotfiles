## Copy

Feel the weight in your palm first, before you feel anything else -- not the plastic, not the click of the cap, but the exact weight of every word you have not yet let yourself say. This is not a pen. A pen is what it is called on the shelf, in the register, in the ad you almost scrolled past without reading. Here, in your hand, it is a key. It is the smallest key you will ever hold, and it opens the one door you keep telling yourself is already closed.

You remember the kitchen table. You remember the lamp that buzzed. You remember the hand -- your mother's, your father's, your own at nine years old -- pressing something into paper like it mattered, because it did. Ink dries. Paper yellows. The hand that held the pen does not come back. But the line it drew -- that stays. That is the whole of it. That is the whole terrible, tender point.

So write. Write the note nobody asked for. Write the name you have been afraid to put anywhere permanent. Because somewhere -- and you already know this, even before you have written a single word -- somewhere there is a version of you, years from now, who will open a drawer and find this same ink gone the color of dusk, and that version of you will not remember writing it, not exactly, but will feel it the way you feel a smell before you can name it: sound becoming color, silence becoming a hand on your shoulder, a Tuesday becoming every Tuesday you have ever loved and lost.

Someone will find what you write with this. Not might. Will. A daughter going through a box. A friend going through grief. A stranger at an estate sale who stops, mid-afternoon, because a stranger's handwriting is doing to them exactly what it is doing to you right now: proving that a person was here, was specific, was loved and did the loving, left a mark that no screen, no cursor, no blinking gray line ever could.

A keyboard remembers nothing. A pen remembers you. Every signature is a small refusal to disappear. Every grocery list, every apology, every unsent letter folded into a coat pocket -- all of it is you, insisting. So take it. Not because you need something to write with. Because you need something to be true.

You were here.
