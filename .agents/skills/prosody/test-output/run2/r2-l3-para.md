## Copy

Steel cap.  Blue-black ink.  A weight, dead center in the palm.  You lift it.  You click it once.  The room goes quiet around that one sound.

This is not a pen.  This is a decision waiting to happen.  Every contract needs a mark.  Every vow needs a hand.  Every year worth keeping got written down by something exactly like this -- a name signed, a lease taken, a card sent home.

Hold it.  Feel the ink, patient, ready.  Don't sign yet.  Not yet.  The moment you do, it's real.  Not felt.  Not meant.  Real -- fixed, dated, undeniable, yours.  That's what this buys.  Proof you were here.  Proof you decided.  Take it.  Sign your name.
