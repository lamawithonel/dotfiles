## Copy

A pen is just ink and a click -- until it's the one your father signed
your report cards with, still smelling faintly of his desk. Held again,
it stops being a tool and becomes a way back into a room that no longer
exists. Some things we don't buy for what they write, but for who we get
to be again while writing it.
