## Copy

A pen is not an instrument. A pen is a decision that agreed to be held.

Ink does not forget. Paper does not lie. Every signature is a vow written in the one language that cannot be quietly edited afterward.

You did not come here for plastic and a spring. You came here because somewhere a contract, a vow, a scrawled address on a napkin needed a hand steady enough to make it real.

If you do not trust your memory, trust your hand. If you do not trust your hand, give it something worth trusting. This is that thing: brass weighted to your grip, a nib ground to the angle of your own conviction, ink that dries the instant you mean it.

Feel the click. Feel the drag of tip against the tooth of the paper, the small resistance that proves something is actually happening. Smell the ink, faint and mineral, the smell every important document has worn since long before screens.

The world moved onto glass. Nothing that mattered ever did.

Every deed, every will, every vow at an altar still waits on one thing: a hand, a pen, a name. Buy the pen. Not because you need to write. Because someday, something will need you to mean it.
