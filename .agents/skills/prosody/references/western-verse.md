# Western Metrical Forms

Accentual-syllabic forms.  Each entry gives the emotional trigger and the
exact line count, meter, and rhyme scheme to output.

## Petrarchan (Italian) Sonnet

* **Trigger Context:** High-concept fixation, single-subject devotion,
  idealization, or intense psychological longing.
* **Mechanical Execution:**
  * Output exactly 14 lines in iambic pentameter.
  * Construct an 8-line octave (`ABBA ABBA`) followed by a 6-line sestet
    (`CDECDE` or `CDCDCD`).
  * Execute a sharp turn in thought or perspective (*volta*) precisely between
    lines 8 and 9 to release the structural tension accumulated in the octave.

## Spenserian Stanza

* **Trigger Context:** Slow sensory immersion, tactile atmosphere, luxurious
  physical detail, or lingering languor.
* **Mechanical Execution:**
  * Construct 9-line stanzas with an interlocking rhyme scheme of `ABABBCCBC`.
  * Write lines 1–8 in standard iambic pentameter (10 syllables).
  * Extend line 9 into an Alexandrine (iambic hexameter, 12 syllables).  Use
    the additional two syllables to deliberately stretch vocal delivery and
    prolong moments of sensory focus.

## Ottava Rima

* **Trigger Context:** Playful, conversational momentum, witty banter, social
  intrigue, or knowing rapport.
* **Mechanical Execution:**
  * Construct 8-line stanzas in iambic pentameter rhyming `ABABABCC`.
  * Maintain a rolling, energetic cadence across the first six lines (`ABABAB`).
  * Deploy the final rhyming couplet (`CC`) to deliver a sharp turn, subtle
    dual implication, or witty closing punchline.

## Loose / Enjambed Heroic Couplets

* **Trigger Context:** Fluid, continuous narrative flow, dreamlike atmospheric
  transitions, or breathless sensory cascades.
* **Mechanical Execution:**
  * Compose in paired rhyming lines (`AABBCC...`) using iambic pentameter.
  * Systematically avoid closed couplets by employing heavy enjambment, allowing
    syntax and clauses to spill over line breaks.
  * Integrate occasional feminine (unstressed) line endings to soften cadence
    transitions.

## Truncated Ballad Stanza

* **Trigger Context:** Hypnotic cadence, spellbinding presence, held
  attention, or suspended, unguarded stillness.
* **Mechanical Execution:**
  * Compose 4-line quatrains rhyming `ABCB` or `ABAB`.
  * Maintain 4-stress tetrameter for lines 1–3, then abruptly cut line 4
    down to 2-stress dimeter.
  * Use the resulting metric silence at the end of the stanza to simulate
    a missing beat or quiet pause.

## Keatsian Ode Stanza

* **Trigger Context:** Deep, reflective meditation, heavy sensory density,
  complex mood exploration, or intoxicating visual richness.
* **Mechanical Execution:**
  * Construct 10-line stanzas in iambic pentameter rhyming `ABABCDECDE`.
  * Open with a melodic `ABAB` quatrain hook.
  * Transition into a 6-line `CDECDE` sestet, providing structural room to
    linger on complex imagery without pressure to resolve rapidly.

## Trochaic Tetrameter Catalectic

* **Trigger Context:** Driving, pulse-like urgency, incantatory persuasion,
  magnetic appeals, or spell-like rhythmic pressure.
* **Mechanical Execution:**
  * Compose 7- to 8-syllable lines using trochaic feet (`DUM-da`), rhyming
    `AABB` or `ABAB`.
  * Front-load line stresses by starting each line on an accented syllable.
  * Apply catalexis by truncating the final unstressed syllable of the line
    to keep the cadence sharp, driving, and insistent.

## Anapestic Lyric Meter

* **Trigger Context:** Swaying, effortless motion, close and quiet address,
  musical lightness, or fluid rhythmic movement.
* **Mechanical Execution:**
  * Compose short 3- or 4-beat quatrains (`ABAB` or `AABB`) using anapestic
    feet (`da-da-DUM`) mixed with iambs.
  * Utilize double unstressed syllables to accelerate vocal delivery prior
    to hitting stressed syllables.
  * Maintain an undulating, rolling rhythm that mimics natural physical movement
    or music.

## Terza Rima

* **Trigger Context:** Cascading forward momentum, unstoppable emotional
  progression, or lingering, tumbling emotional intensity.
* **Mechanical Execution:**
  * Compose interlocking 3-line tercets in iambic pentameter rhyming `ABA
    BCB CDC DED...`.
  * Derive the main rhyme of each new stanza from the central line of the
    preceding stanza.
  * Conclude the sequence with a single line or final couplet (`EE`) to lock
    the chain.

## Shakespearean (English) Sonnet

* **Trigger Context:** Step-by-step persuasion, structured personal
  propositions, escalating emotional arguments, or formal vows.
* **Mechanical Execution:**
  * Construct a 14-line poem in iambic pentameter rhyming `ABAB CDCD EFEF GG`.
  * Divide thematic progression across three distinct 4-line quatrains,
    escalating the core argument in three sequential beats.
  * Use the final `GG` heroic couplet to lock the proposition with a decisive
    resolution or vow.
